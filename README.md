# ABconsent — Google, Meta & OpenAI Consent Mode for Google Tag Manager

Load the **ABconsent** consent banner on your website straight from Google Tag Manager.
No code, no developer, no extra script to install.

**[Create your CMP on abconsent.com](https://www.abconsent.com)** — a free plan is available.

## What this template does

Add the tag, enter your partner and configuration IDs, and it takes care of the rest:

- **Loads the ABconsent CMP** — the consent banner your visitors see, and the choices it records.
- **Google Consent Mode** — your Google tags automatically receive the visitor's choice.
- **Meta (Facebook) Consent Mode** — your Meta Pixel receives it too.
- **OpenAI Consent Mode** — same for the OpenAI measurement pixel.

Each signal is sent before your other tags fire, so every vendor knows what it is allowed to do
from the very first page view.

## Get started

1. [Create your account](https://www.abconsent.com) and set up your banner.
2. Add this template to your GTM container.
3. Enter your partner ID and configuration ID.
4. Publish.

[Read the documentation](https://cmp.docs.sirdata.net/v/en/script-management/google-consent-mode)
